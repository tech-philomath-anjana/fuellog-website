Delete your account

Last updated 13 October 2026

You can have your FuelLog account deleted at any time. You do not need the station owner's permission.

If you are the owner, download your days first. Open Days and export them. Once the account is deleted, there is no way to sign back in and fetch them.

How to ask

  1. Email mahasathian.anjana@gmail.com from an address we can reply to.
  2. Tell us the phone number your FuelLog account uses.
  3. We confirm the account is yours before deleting anything.
  4. We delete it within 30 days and let you know when it is done.

Deletion cannot be undone. Signing in with the same number afterwards starts a new, empty account.

You will also be able to delete your account from inside the app once the Android app is released.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in. That number no longer reaches this account.
  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your access to the station

What is kept

The station's financial records are kept: the meter readings, rates, cash figures, expenses and credit entries made while you used FuelLog.

Your name and number are removed from them, so the entries no longer identify you. They remain as the station's record of what it sold and received.

If you are the station's only owner

Deleting your account closes the station for everyone.

Every member of staff at that station is switched off at the same time. They can still sign in, but they cannot open the station or enter another day. Each of them can ask for their own account to be deleted too.

There is currently no way to hand a station over to someone else. If you want the station to carry on, do not delete your account.
