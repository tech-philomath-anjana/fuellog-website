How it works

Three steps at the end of the day.

1

Enter the closing readings

Type each pump's closing reading on the counter computer. Soon your manager will also be able to enter it at the pump on the Android app. The opening is carried from last night.

2

Add what came in and went out

GPay and card, fuel given on credit, cash paid back, and expenses. FuelLog works out the cash to hand over as you go.

3

Hand in, and you approve

The day is handed in. You check it on the counter computer and approve it with your PIN. After that it is sealed.

Questions

What owners ask first.

How much does it cost?

The first 15 days are free. For pricing after that, email us and we'll reply with the price for your station.

What happens when the 15 days are up?

The station locks and everyone is signed out until it is paid for. Nothing is deleted: your days, readings and credit are all still there when it reopens.

What do I need to run it?

A Windows computer and an internet connection. Download the installer, sign in with your phone number, and set up your fuels, tanks and pumps.

Is there a mobile app?

An Android app is coming soon, for you and your staff. It will be included at no extra cost for every station on FuelLog for Windows.

What if the internet drops?

Each figure is saved as soon as you move to the next box, and FuelLog needs the internet to do it. If a save fails, that box is marked and the day can't be handed in until it is saved, so nothing goes missing quietly.

Does my manager need training?

The screens follow the order of the day book they already keep: readings, rates, money in, money out. A wrong entry is pointed out on the spot, in plain words.

Who can see the cash figures?

Only people you've approved into your station. Staff see the days they work; you see everything.

What if a figure was wrong after I approved it?

You can correct it. The change is recorded and shown on that day, with who made it and when.

Which fuels and pumps does it handle?

Petrol, diesel and any other fuel you sell, across your tanks, pumps and nozzles. Add more or switch them off any time. Lubricants and other counter goods aren't in the day book yet, so a station that sells them still totals those separately.
