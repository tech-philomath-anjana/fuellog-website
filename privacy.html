Privacy policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

FuelLog is software for recording a fuel station's daily trade. It runs on Windows, and an Android app is on the way. This policy explains what FuelLog collects, who else sees it, and how to have it deleted.

Who we are

FuelLog is published by an individual developer in Tamil Nadu, India. Contact: mahasathian.anjana@gmail.com.

What we collect

To sign you in: your phone number and your name. There is no password. We send a one-time code to your number instead.

The station's records: what is entered while running the day, such as meter readings, fuel rates, money received and paid out, stock measurements and amounts owed on account. Each entry is linked to the account that made it and the time it was made, and later changes are recorded too, so the station's owner can see who recorded what.

Credit customers' details: if the station sells fuel on account, it enters its customers' names and phone numbers, and the registration number of the vehicle each fill went into. We store this for the station and use it for nothing else. The station decides what to record and answers its own customers about it.

The camera, in the Android app, if your station reads pump meters by photograph. The reading is taken from the photo on your phone. The photo is not uploaded.

What is kept on your computer

The Windows app remembers the people who use it on that computer, so they can switch with a four-digit PIN instead of a new code each time. For each person it keeps their name, their role, and their sign-in, locked with their PIN. Signing out on that computer removes that person from it.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising, and no advertising networks
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No location
  • No access to your contacts

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing.

Who we share it with

These companies are needed for FuelLog to work:

  • Supabase hosts the database and handles sign-in. The station's records are stored on its servers in Mumbai, India.
  • Twilio sends your one-time codes by text message. It receives your phone number and the code in order to send it. Twilio is based in the United States, so this step leaves India.
  • Resend sends us an email when somebody creates a new fuel station in FuelLog, so we know who is using it. The email contains the station's name and the owner's name and phone number. Resend is based in the United States, so this step leaves India. It receives nothing about the station's daily records.
  • Google Cloud stores our backups of the station's records, on servers in Mumbai, India. Backups are encrypted before they leave our computer, and Google does not have the key.
  • GitHub hosts the Windows installer and its updates. When the app checks for or downloads an update, GitHub sees your computer's internet address. It receives none of the station's records.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like any web host, it sees the internet address of each visitor.

Nobody else.

How we protect it

Each person can only reach their own station. Staff see the day they are working on and the days they entered. The owner sees their whole station. Data is encrypted in transit. We do not write names or phone numbers into diagnostic logs.

Deleting your account

To delete your account, open Account in the Windows app and choose Delete my account. We send a code to your phone to check it is you, and the account is deleted as soon as you type it in.

You can also email mahasathian.anjana@gmail.com from an address we can reply to, and tell us the phone number your account uses. We check that the account is yours, then delete it within 30 days.

Deleted: your sign-in, your name, your phone number and your access to the station.

Kept: the station's financial records, such as readings, rates, cash figures, expenses and credit entries, with your name and number removed so they no longer identify you. These are the station's record of its own trade.

If you are the station's only owner, deleting your account closes the station for everyone who works there.

The account deletion page has the full details.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask what we hold about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted. Write to mahasathian.anjana@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

If a station recorded you as a credit customer, ask that station first, since they entered your details. Write to us if you cannot reach them.

Children

FuelLog is for running a business and is not intended for anyone under 18.
